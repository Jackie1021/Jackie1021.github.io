Psycho  AI真人心灵社交平台

AI-driven social networking platform · Founder: Jackie Chen

Psycho is dedicated to revolutionizing traditional social networking by creating meaningful, soulful connections — helping people find their true soulmate through AI-powered authentic human interaction. Founded by Jackie Chen, a '985' Physics PhD dropout, ex-algorithm engineer, and current PhD researcher at HKU.

Total Funding Raised to Date: HK$ 0.9 Million
Dec 10, 2025 🏆 Techathon+ 10th Anniversary Finals — Merit Award

Psycho competed in the finals of the Hong Kong Techathon+, one of Hong Kong's most prestigious university-enterprise innovation competitions, co-organised by HKSTP and 20+ top universities. Psycho won the Merit Award in the Trusted AI & Data Science (Student Group) track.

Techathon+ is the flagship innovation challenge jointly run by Hong Kong Science and Technology Parks Corporation (HKSTP) and leading Hong Kong universities, drawing hundreds of teams across 4 cutting-edge technology tracks. Winning a merit award at the finals represents formal recognition from one of HK's top innovation ecosystems.

Feb 10, 2026 🎓 Selected into HKU Techno-Entrepreneurship Core Incubation Programme

Psycho was selected into the HKU Techno-Entrepreneurship Core (TEC) startup incubation programme, gaining access to shared workspace at HKU, mentorship from seasoned entrepreneurs and investors, and a collaborative founder network within Asia's top university innovation hub.

Mar 10, 2026 💻 Shortlisted into Cyberport Incubation Programme Finals

Psycho advanced to the final stage of the Cyberport Creative Micro Fund (CCMF) incubation programme — Hong Kong's government-backed flagship digital technology hub. Cyberport has incubated over 2,000 startups and its alumni include billion-dollar companies such as WeLab (digital banking unicorn) and GoGoX (logistics tech). Being shortlisted to the finals puts Psycho among the most competitive tech ventures in Hong Kong.

Apr 22, 2026 🚀 HKSTP Ideation Programme — HK$100,000 Seed Funding (0% equity)

Psycho received a HK$100,000 seed grant (non-dilutive — 0% equity taken) from the Round 1 of the HKSTP Ideation Programme and was officially admitted into the HKSTP incubator ecosystem.

HKSTP (Hong Kong Science and Technology Parks) is the premier government-backed I&T hub of Hong Kong, managing Science Park, InnoCentre, and industrial estates. The Ideation Programme is one of its most selective pre-seed tracks, with notable alumni including Animoca Brands (Web3 unicorn), Klook (travel-tech unicorn, $1.2B valuation), and numerous AI and biotech ventures. Being selected with a non-equity grant is a strong signal of product-market fit potential.

May 1, 2026 📱 Psycho Enters First Round of User Beta Testing

Psycho officially launched its closed beta testing phase with the first cohort of real users — a critical milestone validating product readiness and gathering early-stage UX and retention data to inform the next iteration.
