Psycho  AI真人心灵社交平台

AI-driven social networking platform · Founder: Jackie Chen

Psycho is dedicated to revolutionizing traditional social networking — creating meaningful, soulful connections and helping people find their true soulmate through AI-powered authentic human interaction. Founded by Jackie Chen, a '985' Physics PhD dropout, ex-algorithm engineer, and current PhD researcher at HKU.

Total Funding Raised to Date  ·  HK$ 900,000  ≈ USD 115K
Dec 10, 2025 🏆 Hong Kong Techathon+ 10th Anniversary Finals — Merit Award

Psycho competed in the Hong Kong Techathon+ — one of HK's most prestigious university-enterprise innovation competitions, co-organised by HKSTP and 20+ top universities including HKU, HKUST, CUHK, and PolyU.

Psycho won the Merit Award in the Trusted AI & Data Science (Student Group) track — formal recognition from Hong Kong's flagship I&T ecosystem.

Feb 10, 2026 🎓 HKU Techno-Entrepreneurship Core — Incubation Programme

Psycho was selected into the HKU Techno-Entrepreneurship Core (TEC) startup incubation programme, gaining access to shared workspace at HKU, mentorship from seasoned entrepreneurs and investors, and a collaborative founder network within Asia's top university innovation hub.

Mar 10, 2026 💻 Cyberport Incubation Programme — Shortlisted to Finals

Psycho advanced to the final stage of the Cyberport Creative Micro Fund (CCMF) — Hong Kong's government-backed flagship digital technology hub. Cyberport has incubated 2,000+ startups, with alumni including unicorns WeLab (digital banking) and GoGoX (logistics tech).

Apr 22, 2026 🚀 HKSTP Ideation Programme — HK$100,000 Seed Grant (0% equity)

Psycho received a HK$100,000 non-dilutive seed grant in Round 1 of the HKSTP Ideation Programme and was officially admitted into the HKSTP incubator ecosystem.

HKSTP is HK's premier government-backed I&T hub managing Science Park, InnoCentre, and industrial estates. The Ideation Programme is one of its most selective pre-seed tracks — notable alumni include Animoca Brands (Web3 unicorn) and Klook (travel-tech, $1.2B valuation).

May 1, 2026 📱 Psycho Launches First Round of Closed Beta Testing

Psycho officially entered its closed beta testing phase with real users — a key milestone validating product-market fit and collecting early UX and retention data for the next product iteration.
